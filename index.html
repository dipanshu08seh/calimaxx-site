Good to know.

Is CaliMaxx for beginners?

Yes. Your assessment sets a separate level for each movement, so your plan starts where you are.

Do I need a gym?

No. CaliMaxx builds your plan around the equipment you have, whether you train at home, at the park, or in a gym. Some goals need a bar, rings, or other equipment.

Is a subscription required?

Yes. After onboarding and your plan reveal, a CaliMaxx Pro subscription is required to train. Available plans, local prices, and any applicable offers are shown in the app before you purchase. Read the subscription terms.

What does Apple Health sync?

With your permission, CaliMaxx saves completed workouts and the body weight you log to Apple Health, and reads body weight from Health. You can turn the connection off in the app and manage permissions in Apple Health. See how Health data is handled.
