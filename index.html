I can't do a push-up yet. Is it for me?

Yes. Your assessment sets a separate level for each movement, so your plan starts where you are, even before your first push-up.

Do I need a gym?

No. Kalstheno builds your plan around the equipment you have, at home, at the park or in a gym. Some skills need a bar, rings or parallettes, and the app tells you which.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. Everything works on iPhone. With a watch you can follow the workout, log sets and run timers from your wrist.

Is a subscription required?

Yes. After onboarding and your plan reveal, a Kalstheno Pro subscription is required to train. Plans and local prices are shown in the app before you buy. Read the subscription terms.

What does Apple Health sync?

With your permission, Kalstheno saves completed workouts and the body weight you log to Apple Health, and reads body weight from it. You can turn the connection off at any time. See how Health data is handled.
